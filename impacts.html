Environmental impacts

Vegetation decline weakens the whole ecosystem

Habitat loss, biodiversity decline, waterway degradation, erosion, and reduced carbon storage are connected consequences of vegetation loss.

01

Habitat loss

Land clearance removes nesting, shelter, food sources, and movement corridors.

02

Biodiversity decline

Native species struggle when connected ecosystems become smaller and more isolated.

03

Waterway degradation

Sediment and stormwater runoff increase when riparian vegetation is reduced.

04

Reduced resilience

Less carbon storage, more erosion, and less shade increase climate vulnerability.
