Protecting South Auckland's Vegetation
Issue Why Where Action

Solutions for the future

South Auckland can grow without losing what protects it

The strongest solutions combine native planting, green space protection, sustainable planning, environmental education, and community involvement.

Native planting

Restore stream edges, reserves, and habitat corridors with native species suited to each site.

Protect green spaces

Safeguard mature trees, wetlands, parks, and ecological corridors before development begins.

Sustainable urban planning

Design housing, roads, and infrastructure around waterways, canopy cover, and biodiversity.

Environmental education

Help students and communities identify native species, pest plants, and restoration opportunities.
